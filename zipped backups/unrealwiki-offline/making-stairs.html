Making Stairs

Types of stairs builder

  • linear
  • curved
  • spiral (and TarquinSpiralStairBuilder)

Use semisolids

Forum snip from Tarquin:

Create stairs with semisolids. You'll save on a ton of BSP cuts. Though be careful with the Semisolid Region Limit Error (made-up name ) – avoid having visible semisolid faces flush with the containing subtract box. (ie, your box is 4 steps high – add a 3-step semisold staircase and cleverly texture the side of the box to look like the last step).

Stylistically, more than 8 or so stairs is a bad idea. It doesn't look good, and when a player runs up them their view goes de-dum-dedum-dedum-dedum.

The method in the upper diagram is better than the lower diagram, as the semisolid face marked with a * may vanish when looking at the stairs from the front.

Dimensions & Aesthetics

The the maximum height each step can have and still be passable without jumping is 32 Unreal Units. Steps are usually built 16 units high. (see General Scale And Dimensions for more).
