Global Domination/Features

  • Voting System
    • Vote for Commander
    • Vote to Kick Player
  • Command Interface
    • Smooth panning RTS-style camera
    • Unit Selection
      • Selection Pad
      • Navigation Points
        • Assign player to move to Nav Point
        • Destruction of Nav Point when player reaches it
    • Building Selection
    • Unit and Building Information (Health, score etc.)
  • Team Play
    • All Players of Team spawn at same location
