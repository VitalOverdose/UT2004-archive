ONSManualGun

UT2004 :: Actor >> ONSWeapon >> ONSManualGun

Summary

This is the fire class of the Energy Turret, This sets gfx properties and implements the damage dealing firepower doled out by our energy turret. This class is referenced in ONSManualGunPawn.

Something to note about all ONSWeapon sub-classes is that they only can fire when in one of the firing states. InstantFireMode or ProjectileFireMode. This takes place through the ONSWeapon property bInstantFire which is checked in ONSWeapon's PostNetBeginPlay() function to goto one of the two states.

EXECS

  1. exec OBJ LOAD FILE=..\Animations\ONSWeapons-A.ukx
  2. exec OBJ LOAD FILE=..\Textures\TurretParticles.utx

This performs a trace from point a to point b and applies (DamageMin + Rand(DamageMax - DamageMin)) damage to anything in the way.

Checks are made to ensure the person firing is not caught in the trace

Any effects that should be applied to the recieving end of the trace are applied.
