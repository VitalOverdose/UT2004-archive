Global Domination/Concept

Global Domination: Man vs. Machine

Game Concept

Genre: FPS (First Person Shooter), RTS (Real Time Strategy)

Engine: Unreal

Platform: Cross-platform

Game Mechanics

Game Play

  • In the beginning of the game, each side is given a base and either a civilian or a drone.
  • The environment is usually a post-apocalyptic urban center with destroyed buildings and homeless civilians
  • Resources. Humans: You can recruit civilians to help your cause. Fallen comrades can be scavenged for their inventory. Destroyed Vehicles can be scavenged. Destroyed enemy units can be scavenged. Machines: (need primary resource). Destroyed drones and units.
  • Combat. (Applies to both sides) Commander cannot engage in FPS style combat. Instead, he directs his units (6 max controlled by people, 6 max controlled by dummy computer) into combat and controls the non-player units, which can build structures and carry out his dirty work.
  • Units can be "possessed" by people (6 max). A person can only possess a unit if it is not possessed by another person or is not dead.
  • Technology. Humans: EMP shockwave. Machines: Nerve Gas, Interfacing (can convert enemy mechanical units) Both: Plasma Shield (good against energy weapons. doesn’t work against bullets)
  • Goal. Once an enemy base is destroyed, that side becomes defeated.

Game Elements

  • Commander/Base - Controls and instructs all subordinates

Humans:

  • Civilians - Can build structures, lay mines and traps. Can be upgraded into infantry by going into the barracks
  • Infantry - Armored, more equipped version of the civ. Inventory can be customized by either the person who is possessing the unit or the commander
  • Vehicles - Built in the Vehicle Assembly Factory. Armor, upgrades and armament can be customized by commander only
  • Base - must protect
  • VAF - assembles vehicles
  • Sentry guns - self explanatory

Machines:

  • Drones - Can build structures, lay mines and traps. Can be upgraded in the Drone Upgrade Center. Can be upgraded into vehicles in the (I don't know a name yet)
  • Advanced Drones - More sensors. Fitted with weapons and armor.
  • Vehicle Type Drones - Heavy armor. Heavy weaponry (maybe cannons)
  • Base - protect or die
  • Drone Upgrade center - creates Advanced Drones
  • (Some name for making vehicles) - creates Vehicle Type Drones
  • Sentry Guns - self explanatory

Game Strengths

  • Requires teamwork and communication
  • Highly Flexible command interface
  • Many different strategies (adds to the replay value)
  • Many gametypes (KoTH, CTC, Coop)

Similar Games

  • Natural Selection for Half-Life
