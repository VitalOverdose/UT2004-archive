Vehicle Gameplay

This is a work in progress...

The aim of this page is to help mappers and effectively players pick the right vehicle to place in the right place of a map, that will compliment the overall play style of the map. I am mainly basing it on my experience of Assault Mapping but please add anything appropriate for other game types.

I feel that the Bonus Pack Vehicles are underrated in many ways but first I will do a summary of all the original vehicles and their strengths and weaknesses.

Hellbender (Jeep)

This is a slightly overused vehicle (not that I'm complaining) but has a bias towards camping. The rear turret causes chaos at long range when fired accurately as it has the effect of 'pushing' vehicles. The EMP cannon causes fatality for all short range infantry due to the large splash damage when triggered. The main weakness is its high driver/passenge damage. If you take a direct hit from a goliath your almost certain to be killed even if the jeep isn't destroyed due to the large splash damage from the shell. The safest positian is manning the EMP turret then driver then rear turret being the worst effected. Unfortunatly this means that all vehicles excluding the manta and scorpion can injure the occupants if the right moment and place is chosen. (mainly because of the large splash damage created by the projectiles.

  • More likely of sucess : Choke points (tendancy to camp though) and areas where there will be players on foot.
  • Less likely of sucess : Open areas when flying vehicles or larger wheeled vehicles are nearby

Goliath (Tank)

The main disadvantages of the goliath is its speed and more importantly the main cannon's turning speed. The driver recieves no damage while in the vehicle. It is effective against all of the wheeled vehicles and in a tunnel pretty much invincible.

  • More likely of sucess : Choke points (tendancy to camp though) where there is only one direction of attack (cannon turning speed)
  • Less likely of sucess : Open areas with flying vehicles or mantas and where attacks can come from mltiple sides.
