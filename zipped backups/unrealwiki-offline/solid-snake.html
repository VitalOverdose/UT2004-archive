Journal

Chaos Engine

Chaos Engine is a platform for UT2004 designed to make it easier to make mods from. At first this started out as a simple RPG project, but I started to make my classes more and more broad and finally ended up as a platform of core classes with modules that could dynamically run the core on the fly. Chaos Engine currently powers two modules that are in production by UArchitect, Aegeri and myself. These are currently called Unreal Leagues (Nick named UT2006) and a unnamed 2D RPG Project.

Design Documents

I've finally admitted it. I tend to get a lot of ideas and often these ideas fancy my mind for a while, I start coding it and then another idea hits me and thus I drop the previous idea and ... well it is a vicious cycle and it tends to happen a lot. Thus I've decided that before I start any new ideas that I should discuss the ideas and overtime I will figure out if the idea was any good to begin with ... without writing a line of code, or adding a single brush to an empty map!

Technical reviews

Technical reviews of mods are looking at the technical aspects of mods. These reviews aren't focused on how fun I think the game is, or how good I think it will be, but rather how well these mod teams have created a mod. It covers things from the code side of things, to the functionality of the mod. Its mainly designed for the mod developers who made the mod to see what areas they may wish to have a look at in order to achieve certain things such as increasing the functionality of their UI for example. Some people who are learning how to use Unreal as a modding tool as such may also benefit from reading these reviews.
